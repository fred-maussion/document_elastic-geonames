Demo 01 · lead story

Go from the world to a single room.

The workplace team wants to know where the tablets are, how many, and in which building, without asking for an export.

Pain

Estate questions need a spreadsheet.

The registry lists tablets, but nobody can see the estate by country, city or building at a glance.

Elastic approach

Drill down on one live map.

Country shading at world level, city bubbles when you zoom in, then buildings and a room table, filtered by country or site.

One view that stays current.
