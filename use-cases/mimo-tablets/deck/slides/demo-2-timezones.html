Demo 02

Plan support and rollouts across 22 time zones.

An app update or restart should never land in the middle of an office day. The registry has no time zone; the enrichment adds one.

Pain

Rollouts are planned by hand.

Without a time zone per office, nobody can tell which offices are open when an update goes out.

Elastic approach

Group the fleet by time zone.
